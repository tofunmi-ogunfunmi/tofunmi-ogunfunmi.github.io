Experience

Aug 2021 to present

Graduate Researcher, Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory

National Center for Electron Microscopy, advised by Dr. Mary Scott
Ag/Li₃PS₄ solid-state electrolytes
  • Led multimodal characterization (SEM/EDS, FIB tomography) of Ag-nanoparticle-filled Li₃PS₄ electrolytes to establish the structure–property relationships behind dendrite suppression.
  • Showed that dispersing Ag nanoparticles in amorphous Li₃PS₄ suppresses lithium dendrite failure, extending cell lifetime from ~18 h to over 80 h with stable cycling.
4D-STEM analysis pipeline
  • Built Python pipelines (py4DSTEM, NumPy, scikit-learn) that process GB-scale 4D-STEM datasets into phase maps and defect signatures.
  • Developing automated stacking-fault detection with PCA and supervised classification to replace manual review.
Beam–material interactions and high-throughput testing
  • Investigated electron-beam damage in LLZO using air-free transfer; contributed to high-throughput conductivity-screening protocols for accelerated materials qualification.
  • Performed ex-situ TEM/SEM of LLZO supporting work showing that beam charging drives lithium growth on solid-electrolyte surfaces, setting criteria to separate real electrochemistry from imaging artifacts.

Hardware Developer Intern, IBM

Chemical & X-ray Failure Analysis, Fishkill, NY
  • Ran root-cause failure analysis on field-returned Li-ion battery leakage with 3D X-ray and SEM/EDS, localizing the leak to the gasket and identifying corrosion- and electrolyte-derived chemistry.
