About me

Materials scientist exploring how solid-state batteries fail, and how to make them last.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Materials Science & Engineering at UC Berkeley, where I work at the National Center for Electron Microscopy at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab with Dr. Mary Scott.

I use electron microscopy methods and materials data analysis to understand solid-state battery materials: why electrolytes short and crack, and what makes them more durable.

Research

My research focuses on solid-state batteries, which replace the flammable liquid in today's lithium-ion cells.

I study how the solid-state microstructure controls battery performance, using electron microscopy methods (e.g. SEM/EDS, TEM/STEM, 4D-STEM, EELS, FIB-SEM tomography).
